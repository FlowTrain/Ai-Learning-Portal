# Staircase Map — Every Concept × Three Rungs

Generated by the full-catalog staircase audit (2026-07-10, BACKLOG 2). Rungs per `docs/program-architecture.md`: 1 = plain language, 2 = patterned language, 3 = structured format. Rule: a rung-3 lesson may not cite a concept as prerequisite unless that concept exists at rung 1.

Two kinds of rung-1 coverage appear below: **standalone** (a rung-1 lesson) and **embedded** (a plain-language opening activity inside a rung-2 lesson — the pattern established in the BACKLOG 1 rewrite, legitimate for concepts too small to carry a full Tier 1 lesson).

**No holes** — every concept has coverage at all three rungs. Coverage strength notes:

- **A0 was carrying two concept rows by itself.** Until 2026-07-17, "Safety & governance" *and*
  "Checking AI output" both listed their rung-1 ancestor as **A0 (The One Rule)** — two of the most
  important Tier 1 concepts compressed into one sub-section of a 15-minute hook lesson. **A5 and A6 now
  own them standalone**; A0 keeps the one-line hook. This was the Level 1 depth expansion's core fix
  (cohort feedback: *"not enough Level 1 content — people don't believe they'll make it past this
  stage"*).
- **Noticing where AI fits** had **no row at all**, despite `maturity-model.md` naming *"often forgets
  AI could help"* as a defining Tier 1 observable and **habit formation** as a Tier 1 program pillar.
  A7 creates the ancestor. Nothing above it was orphaned by building it.
- **Prompt reuse, rung 1** was embedded-only; **A7's artifact is A2's opening activity done properly
  and kept**, which repairs it. Watch whether A2 still needs its opening once cohorts arrive via A7.
- **Multi-step workflows, rung 1** is still embedded-only. If Tier 1 cohort feedback shows learners
  hitting A3 unprepared, promote the opening activity to a standalone rung-1 micro-lesson. *(The
  2026-07-15 feedback was about Level 1 volume, not A3 readiness — this trigger has not fired.)*
- **Tool selection & cost, rung 3** is covered via D2/D3 metric specs, which is thinner than a dedicated
  structured treatment. The `token-economics` Evolution Library entry (published 2026-07-10) now holds
  the numbers; C5 carries the rung-2 decision pattern.

## Maintenance

Re-run this audit whenever a lesson is added or a `staircaseRung` changes. The validation script checks frontmatter; this map is the human-readable ancestry record. If a new rung-3 lesson can't fill in its ancestor row, the lesson is premature — build the ancestor first.
