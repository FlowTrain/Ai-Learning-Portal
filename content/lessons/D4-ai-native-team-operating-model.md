# AI-Native Team Operating Model

> The final lesson in the AI-Native track defines what it means to operate as an AI-native team — not just have AI tools available, but have AI embedded into every workflow, every decision process, and every hire. This is the operating model design, not the tool selection.

## AI in the Team Operating Rhythm

An AI-native team has AI integrated at every cadence: in sprint planning (AI-assisted story refinement, AC generation), in code review (AI pre-review before human review), in incident response (AI-drafted postmortems within 2 hours), in retrospectives (AI-synthesized team health from the sprint's communication patterns), and in documentation (AI-maintained, human-reviewed). The key word is 'integrated' — AI is a step in the process, not an optional add-on.

## AI in Hiring and Onboarding

AI-native organizations signal their expectations in hiring: job descriptions reference required AI maturity tier, technical screens include an AI workflow exercise, and onboarding has a 30-day AI-Enabled certification requirement. Existing team members who haven't reached AI-Enabled tier have a structured plan to get there. AI maturity is a professional expectation, not a personal hobby.

## What AI-Native Is Not

AI-native is not: using AI for every task, replacing human judgment with AI output, or having the most advanced tools. It is: knowing when AI adds value and when it doesn't, having the infrastructure to use AI reliably at scale, and designing systems where AI failure degrades gracefully rather than catastrophically. The measure of an AI-native team is not how much AI it uses — it's how rarely AI mistakes reach production.

## Assignment

Write the operating model for an AI-native version of your team. Define: the AI-integrated sprint ceremonies, the hiring and onboarding AI requirements, the failure mode protocols, and the quarterly AI infrastructure review. This is a real operational document — not a vision statement.

### Deliverables

- ai-native-operating-model.md: ceremonies, hiring, onboarding, failure protocols, review cadence
- Sprint ceremony design: how AI is integrated at each ceremony (planning, review, retro)
- Hiring criteria: AI maturity requirement per role level (junior, mid, senior, staff)
- Onboarding plan: 30-day AI certification path for new hires
- Failure protocol: what happens when an AI workflow produces a bad output in production
