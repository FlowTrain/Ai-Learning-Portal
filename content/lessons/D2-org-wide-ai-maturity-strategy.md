# Org-Wide AI Maturity Strategy

> AI-Native leaders don't just use AI well — they raise the AI fluency of everyone around them. This lesson builds the organizational strategy for moving a team from AI-Aware to AI-Fluent at scale: metrics, incentive structures, and the learning infrastructure that makes progress visible.

## Maturity as a Business Metric

AI maturity is a lagging indicator of business outcomes. The leading indicators are: what percentage of the team has submitted assignments at each tier? What is the average time to tier promotion? What AI-assisted workflows have been certified team-standard? Tracking these at the org level turns AI upskilling from a training program into a business capability investment with measurable ROI.

## The Infrastructure of Learning

Teams that advance AI maturity fastest share three infrastructure components: a shared skill library (a repository of team-certified AI workflows anyone can use), a prompt quality review process (someone reviews AI-generated outputs before they become canonical), and a context file standard (every project has a CLAUDE.md, maintained, reviewed quarterly). These are engineering infrastructure problems, not training problems.

## Incentive Design

Behavior follows incentives. If AI maturity has no connection to performance reviews, hiring criteria, or team recognition, it won't advance beyond the early adopters. AI-Native organizations make maturity tier visible in team dashboards, link tier progression to continuing education credit requirements, and include AI workflow contribution in engineering promotion criteria. The signal: this isn't optional.

## Assignment

Write an AI maturity strategy for your org or team. Include: the current maturity baseline (by role), the 6-month target state, the three infrastructure investments required, and the metrics you'll use to track progress. This is an executive-level document.

### Deliverables

- ai-maturity-strategy-[team].md: baseline, target, investments, metrics
- Baseline: current tier distribution by role (use real data or honest estimates)
- Target state: specific tier goals per role at 3-month and 6-month marks
- 3 infrastructure investments: what, owner, timeline, success criteria
- Dashboard spec: 5 metrics, measurement method, reporting cadence
