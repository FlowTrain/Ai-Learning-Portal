## 2. The observed shift (why this entry, why now)

Early assumption: AI is too new to be regulated, a compliance gray zone. The shift: regulators declared **technology neutrality** — existing rules (FINRA 3110 supervision, 2210 communications, 4510/SEC 17a-4 recordkeeping, Reg S-P privacy) fully apply to AI-generated activity; the firm owns the output regardless of origin. Practice consequence: HITL review is not a quality nicety but a *named control*; audit trails must link AI outputs to human approvals (21 CFR Part 11 parallel); compliance requirements enter sprints as user stories/NFRs — compliance by design, not by audit.
