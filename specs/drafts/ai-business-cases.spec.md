## 2. The observed shift (why this entry, why now)

How AI work gets justified has moved twice: per-seat tool purchases justified by adoption metrics ("everyone has a license") → per-workflow investment justified by three-term ROI (time − usage − maintenance; the D5 layer) → and now, contested: capability-portfolio funding, where orgs fund platform capabilities (context infrastructure, eval harnesses, data stores) whose value shows up in *other* teams' workflows — the classic platform-funding problem wearing AI clothes. Annual planning is where this bites: adoption theater still wins budget against honest workflow math in most rooms. Track what evidence actually moves funding decisions as the practice matures.
