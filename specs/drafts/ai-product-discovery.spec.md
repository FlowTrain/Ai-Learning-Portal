## 2. The observed shift (why this entry, why now)

AI compressed the *mechanics* of discovery — synthesis in minutes (B2D), instant "user" feedback from synthetic personas, generated interview guides — without compressing the *substance*: contact with real customers. The shift to track: discovery practice split into a productivity arm (AI accelerates synthesis of real evidence — durable win) and a substitution temptation (synthetic users, AI-invented findings — the discovery-cosplay failure mode where teams ship faster while learning nothing). The entry's job is the boundary: where AI acceleration ends and evidence substitution begins.
